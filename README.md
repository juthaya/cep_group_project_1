# CEP146 – Group Project 1

## DeepSeek vs OpenAI: The AI Breakthrough That Shook the Tech Industry

### Project Overview

In January 2025, the release of DeepSeek's AI models challenged assumptions about the cost of developing advanced artificial intelligence. DeepSeek's approach attracted worldwide attention, disrupted technology markets, and raised important questions about the future of AI development.

Our presentation examines DeepSeek's technology, its reported development costs, and its impact on the AI industry.

### Presentation Topics

1. **Introduction to DeepSeek**  
   An overview of DeepSeek's emergence and why its AI models attracted global attention.

2. **DeepSeek's Technology**  
   How DeepSeek-V3 and DeepSeek-R1 use architectural efficiency and reinforcement learning to develop competitive AI capabilities.

3. **AI Development Costs**  
   Examining DeepSeek's reported $5.6 million final training-run compute cost and how it compares with conventional AI development expenses.

4. **Impact on Financial Markets**  
   The January 2025 market reaction, including Nvidia's approximately $593 billion single-day loss in market capitalization.

5. **Implications for Software Development**  
   How efficient, open-weight AI models could reduce barriers to entry, encourage innovation, and expand access to advanced AI tools.

6. **Conclusion and Future Outlook**  
   What DeepSeek's breakthrough could mean for AI competition, development costs, and the future of the technology industry.
