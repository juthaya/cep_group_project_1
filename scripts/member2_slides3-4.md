## Slide 3 — How DeepSeek Reduced Training Costs

One of DeepSeek's biggest achievements was its reported training efficiency.

According to its technical report, the final pretraining run for DeepSeek-V3 cost approximately 5.6 million US dollars in estimated GPU computing expenses.

Although this figure excludes other development costs, it demonstrated how efficient AI architectures could reduce computational requirements.

## Slide 4 — The Industry's Reaction

DeepSeek's breakthrough also had a significant impact on financial markets.

On January 27, 2025, Nvidia lost approximately 593 billion dollars in market value in a single trading session.

Investors began questioning whether future AI development would require the same massive investments in computing hardware.

This sparked a wider discussion about competition, innovation, and the future economics of artificial intelligence.
