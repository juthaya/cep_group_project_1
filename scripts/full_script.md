## Slide 1 — DeepSeek: The AI Breakthrough

What if developing powerful artificial intelligence didn't require billions of dollars in investment?

For years, companies like OpenAI, Google, and Meta have invested heavily in advanced AI development. But in January 2025, a Chinese AI company called DeepSeek challenged the industry's expectations.

With the release of DeepSeek-R1, the company demonstrated competitive reasoning capabilities while drawing attention to its remarkably efficient development methods.

So how did DeepSeek achieve this, and why did it send shockwaves through the technology industry?

In this presentation, we'll explore DeepSeek's technology, its reported training costs, the reaction from financial markets, and what its breakthrough could mean for the future of software development.

---

## Slide 2 — DeepSeek-V3 vs. DeepSeek-R1

DeepSeek developed two important AI models.

DeepSeek-V3 uses a Mixture-of-Experts architecture, activating only a portion of its parameters for each task to improve efficiency.

DeepSeek-R1 builds on this foundation, using reinforcement learning to strengthen its reasoning and problem-solving abilities.

Together, these models demonstrated that advanced AI performance could be achieved through smarter training techniques, not simply greater spending.

## Slide 3 — How DeepSeek Reduced Training Costs

One of DeepSeek's biggest achievements was its reported training efficiency.

According to its technical report, the final pretraining run for DeepSeek-V3 cost approximately 5.6 million US dollars in estimated GPU computing expenses.

Although this figure excludes other development costs, it demonstrated how efficient AI architectures could reduce computational requirements.

---

## Slide 4 — The Industry's Reaction

DeepSeek's breakthrough also had a significant impact on financial markets.

On January 27, 2025, Nvidia lost approximately 593 billion dollars in market value in a single trading session.

Investors began questioning whether future AI development would require the same massive investments in computing hardware.

This sparked a wider discussion about competition, innovation, and the future economics of artificial intelligence.

## Slide 5 — Impact on Software Development

DeepSeek's breakthrough has important implications for software developers.

By releasing open-weight models, DeepSeek provides developers with more flexibility to build and customize AI-powered applications.

Its efficient architecture could also make advanced AI more accessible to smaller companies, independent developers, and students.

For example, developers can explore AI-powered coding assistants, automated workflows, and intelligent applications without relying entirely on expensive proprietary services.

However, security, privacy, and the accuracy of AI-generated results remain important considerations.

---

## Slide 6 — Conclusion

In conclusion, DeepSeek challenged the assumption that developing advanced artificial intelligence always requires enormous financial investments.

Its success highlighted the importance of efficient architectures, innovative training methods, and accessible AI technology.

Ultimately, DeepSeek demonstrated that innovation isn't simply about spending more money, but about finding smarter ways to solve complex problems.
